# Brochure Audit Rules

The supplied brochure is the authoritative source. The auditor must identify defects without redesigning the composition.

## Required audit domains
- Face distortion/anatomy
- AI/generative artifacts
- Color palette consistency
- Typography hierarchy and consistency
- Layout, spacing, clutter and focal competition
- Readability and communication

## Preservation lock
Never rewrite copy, regenerate faces, replace subjects, move major elements, alter logos, or invent a new visual concept. Any repair recommendation must be the minimum change necessary to the existing artwork.

## Report
Each finding must contain: ID, category, severity, confidence, exact region, evidence, impact, minimal correction, preservation risk.

## Important
Basic image statistics alone are insufficient for face, OCR, typography and AI-artifact findings. Those findings require dedicated models or manual verification and must not be hallucinated by the audit engine.
