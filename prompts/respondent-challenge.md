# Respondent-Side Stress Test

Assume the respondent is defending the case.

Test:
- jurisdiction;
- authority;
- sufficiency/specificity of allegations;
- notice/service;
- opportunity to answer;
- investigation/hearing requirements;
- evidence reliability and authenticity;
- chronology;
- nexus to alleged violation;
- elements of the alleged offense;
- penalty authority;
- timeliness/delay;
- impartiality/conflict;
- inconsistent treatment.

Do not invent defenses. Every defense point must identify its record and legal basis.
